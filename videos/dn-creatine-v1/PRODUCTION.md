## 1. Script analysis
**Concept:** cheap filler (not creatine) causes bloat; Nectar is pure and absorbs.
**Audience:** women 40+ who tried creatine, bloated and quit.
**Feeling:** "it wasn't my fault, and here's the clean one."
**Arc:** hook (bloated belly) → why → inside-gut explainer → villain born → hero
packet → USPs → easy ritual → villain defeated → daily life → guarantee → CTA.
