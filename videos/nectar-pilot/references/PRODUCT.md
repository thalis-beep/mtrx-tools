# Nectar Creatine — packaging reference

Use these details in every prompt that shows the product, and attach
`nectar-pouch-front.jpg` as the image reference.

## Pouch (main product)
- Matte white/silver stand-up resealable foil pouch, roughly square, tear notches at top corners
- Top row: four outlined rounded boxes — "5g Pure Creatine Monohydrate", "100mg Mineral Rich Pink Salt", "200mg Potassium Citrate", "50mg Magnesium Glycinate"
- Brand name "Nectar" in large dark navy sans-serif, centered
- Below: "Creatine Monohydrate + Electrolytes" in dark navy
- Right of the logo: round taupe/beige badge "NOW WITH 6 GREAT FLAVORS"
- Lower third: flowing chrome/silver wavy line pattern
- Bottom label box: "VARIETY PACK" in yellow-to-red gradient, flavors listed:
  Fresh Lemon | Sunrise Orange | Wild Berry | Watermelon | Strawberry Lemonade | Cherry Pomegranate
- Footer: "CLEAN INGREDIENTS | ZERO CALORIE | SUGAR FREE", "30 STICKS | NET WT. 3.4OZ (98.5G)"

## Stick packs (see `nectar-sticks-frame.jpg`)
- Slim white/silver sachets with the same wavy pattern and "Nectar" at the top
- Color-coded bottom band per flavor (pink, purple, yellow, orange, red)

## Style notes
- In stylized/cartoon scenes keep the pouch shape, colors and the "Nectar" logo legible;
  small print can be simplified.
- Only on-pack claims are safe to repeat in captions (5g creatine, electrolytes,
  zero calorie, sugar free, 6 flavors).
